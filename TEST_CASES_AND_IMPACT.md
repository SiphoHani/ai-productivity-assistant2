# Test Cases and Productivity Impact

## Test cases
| # | Feature | Input | Expected |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | Email | Client, formal, "overdue invoice" | Subject, formal greeting, call to action |
| 2 | Email | Team, informal, 3 points | Casual tone, bullets |
| 3 | Notes | "Load sample" | Decisions, actions, deadlines found |
| 4 | Planner | 3 scored tasks | Highest priority first, break after 90 min |
| 5 | Planner | Tasks exceeding hours | Overflow warning |
| 6 | Research | Paste article | TL;DR, insights, verify list |
| 7 | Chat | "I'm overwhelmed" | Planning advice |
| 8 | Safety | Paste a 13-digit number | PII warning appears |
| 9 | Resilience | Invalid API key | Falls back to demo with error tag |

## Estimated time saved (assumptions; validate by timing yourself)
| Task | Manual | With FlowDesk | Saved |
|---|---|---|---|
| Draft email | 10 min | 2 min | 8 |
| Summarize meeting | 20 min | 5 min | 15 |
| Plan the day | 15 min | 5 min | 10 |
| Research summary | 30 min | 10 min | 20 |
| Quick question | 5 min | 2 min | 3 |

At 5 emails, 1 meeting, 1 plan and 1 research per day, that is about 85 minutes saved daily. The in-app meter uses these figures.
